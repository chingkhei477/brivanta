How Brivanta works

Everything about eligibility, tracking, approval and withdrawal — in plain language.

The journey

Five steps, start to finish

Step 1

Choose an offer

Browse the Offers page. Check the cashback rate, eligible purchases, confirmation period and terms.

Step 2

Shop through the Brivanta link

Tap “Shop via Brivanta”. The store's website opens and records that you came from Brivanta. Complete the order in that same visit.

Step 3

Submit your claim

Send your store name, order ID, order date and order value through the claim form, from the email address you want updates on.

Step 4

We verify with the store

We match your claim with the store's report. Your cashback stays Pending until the store approves the order after its return window.

Step 5

Withdraw confirmed cashback

Once your confirmed cashback reaches ₹250, request a payout by UPI or bank transfer. We email you when it is sent.

Throughout

Updates by email

We email you whenever your claim changes status. You can ask for an update any time at support@brivanta.in.

Eligibility

Who and what qualifies

Cashback applies only when the store pays a commission for your order. The full rules are in our Cashback Policy.

Your order qualifies when

  • You are 18 or older and shopping from India.
  • You clicked a Brivanta offer link before ordering and completed the order in that visit.
  • The products and payment method are allowed by the offer terms.
  • The order is not cancelled, returned or exchanged.

It usually does not qualify when

  • Another coupon, cashback or rewards site was used after clicking our link.
  • A coupon not listed in the offer terms was applied.
  • The order was placed in the store's app when the offer says website only.
  • The order is for resale, bulk or business purchasing, or breaks the store's rules.

Tracking & approval

What each status means

We use the same five statuses everywhere, and we never mark cashback as Confirmed before the store does.

Claim received

We have your order details and are matching them with the store's report. This can take a few days after your order, depending on the store.

Pending

The store has reported your order. The cashback amount is shown, but it is not yet payable — the store is waiting for its return and cancellation window to close.

Confirmed

The store has approved the order. The cashback is added to your confirmed balance and can be withdrawn once the balance reaches ₹250.

Declined

The store did not approve the order — for example because it was cancelled, returned, not tracked, or outside the offer terms. We share the reason the store gives us.

Paid

Your withdrawal has been sent to the UPI ID or bank account you gave us. We email you the payment reference.

The cashback amount can change between Pending and Confirmed if part of the order is returned or the store adjusts the order value. You always receive the amount the store finally confirms.

Withdrawal

Getting paid

Withdraw confirmed cashback once your balance reaches ₹250.

Request a withdrawal
  • Minimum: ₹250 of confirmed cashback.
  • Methods: UPI, or bank transfer to an Indian bank account in your own name.
  • How: send a withdrawal request from the email address linked to your claims.
  • Checks: we may confirm your payout details by email before sending, to protect your money.
  • Confirmation: we email you the payment reference once the payout is sent, and the status changes to Paid.

Brivanta will never ask for your card number, UPI PIN, net-banking password or OTP. Do not share them with anyone claiming to be from Brivanta.

Order not showing up?

If the store has not reported your order, tell us. We will raise it with the store or its affiliate network. The store makes the final decision.
