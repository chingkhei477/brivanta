How your cashback moves

5 clear stages
  1. Claim receivedYou shopped via a Brivanta link and sent us your order details.
  2. PendingThe store has reported the order and is waiting out its return window.
  3. ConfirmedThe store approved it. The cashback is now yours to withdraw.
  4. PaidSent to your UPI ID or bank account.

If a store cancels or rejects an order, it is marked Declined and we tell you the reason the store gives.

Good to know

Common questions

Where does the cashback come from?

Stores pay a referral commission when someone buys through a partner link. Brivanta passes part of that commission to you as cashback, at the rate shown on the offer.

When can I withdraw?

When your confirmed cashback reaches ₹250 or more. Pending cashback cannot be withdrawn because the store may still cancel or reject the order.

Why does confirmation take time?

Stores wait until their return and cancellation window has closed before approving an order. The expected confirmation period is shown on each offer.

Is cashback guaranteed?

No. Cashback depends on the store tracking and approving your order. Our Cashback Policy lists the common reasons an order may be declined, so you can avoid them.

How do I contact Brivanta?

Email support@brivanta.in or use the contact form. Please never send card numbers, passwords or OTPs.
