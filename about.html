About Brivanta

An India-focused cashback and rewards website, built on one idea: you should always know exactly where your cashback stands.

Who we are

A cashback site that explains itself

Brivanta is a cashback and rewards website for shoppers in India, operated from Bishnupur, Manipur.

When you shop at a partner store through a Brivanta link, the store pays us a referral commission for the sale. We share part of that commission with you as cashback, at the rate shown on the offer.

Cashback can feel confusing — amounts that sit “pending” for weeks, orders that disappear, rules hidden in small print. Brivanta is built to be the opposite: clear offer details, five plain statuses and a real person to write to at support@brivanta.in.

Our principles

How we choose to work

Say it plainly

Rates, eligible purchases, confirmation periods and terms are shown on every offer. If an offer has an important condition, it is not hidden.

Only real offers

We list an offer only when it is active with the store. When an offer ends, it is removed — we do not show expired or made-up deals.

No promises we can't keep

Stores decide whether an order is approved. We never guarantee earnings, and we never show cashback as confirmed before the store does.

Respect for your data

We collect only what is needed to track and pay your cashback, and we explain exactly how in our Privacy Policy.
