Reading an offer

What each detail means

Cashback rate

What you receive — a percentage of the eligible order value or a fixed rupee amount. “Up to” means different products earn different rates.

Eligible purchases

The products, categories or users the store pays on. Anything outside this does not earn cashback.

Confirmation period

Roughly how long the store takes to approve orders after its return window. Your cashback stays Pending until then.

Valid till

The last date the offer applies. Expired offers are removed from this page automatically.

Terms

Store-specific conditions such as coupon rules or excluded payment methods. Please read them before shopping.

Before you click

Help your order track

Tracking is handled by the store. These habits give your order the best chance of being recorded correctly.

  • Start from the Brivanta link and finish the order in the same browser visit.
  • Empty your store cart first, then add items after clicking our link.
  • Allow cookies on the store website and pause ad or tracker blockers for that visit.
  • Use only coupons allowed in the offer terms. Do not visit other coupon or cashback sites in between.
  • Keep your order confirmation email — you will need the order ID for your claim.
